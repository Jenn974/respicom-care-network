5 Signs Your Elderly Parent Needs In‑Home Care

Published April 24, 2026 – by Jane Care Team

Many adult children in Lagos wonder whether their aging parent is safe living alone. Here are five signs that suggest professional home care may be needed.

1. Difficulty with daily tasks

If your parent struggles with bathing, dressing, cooking, or cleaning, they may benefit from a caregiver who can assist while preserving dignity.

2. Memory lapses or confusion

Forgetting medications, appointments, or getting lost in familiar places can be early signs. A trained caregiver can provide reminders and supervision.

3. Recent falls or mobility issues

Falls are a leading cause of injury. Live‑in care can prevent accidents and provide immediate help.

4. Poor nutrition or weight loss

If you notice spoiled food or lost weight, your parent may not be eating properly. Meal preparation is one of our core services.

5. Isolation or depression

Social withdrawal is common. Companionship care can improve mood and keep your loved one engaged.
